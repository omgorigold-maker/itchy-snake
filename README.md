# 🐍 Itchy Snake

**Play it right here (desktop & mobile):** 👉 https://omgorigold-maker.github.io/itchy-snake/

Classic snake, except every 4 seconds it stops to scratch its snaketicals.

## Why?
Because. 

## How to Play
- **Desktop:** Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to steer. Press **Space** or **P** to pause, **R** to restart.
- **Mobile:** **Swipe** on the board or tap the on-screen arrows to steer. **Press and hold** the board to pause (let go to jump right back in).
- **Difficulty:** Starts nice and relaxed, then speeds up the more apples you eat.
- Every 4 seconds, the **Itch Meter** maxes out and the snake pauses for a quick `*scritch scritch*` break, then gets right back to business. You are welcome to do the same – just press the space key (web) or touch and hold the screen (mobile).


Don't hit the walls. Don't bite yourself. Check for cooties. `Itchy Snake™️`.
