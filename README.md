# 🐍 Itchy Snake

**Play it right here (desktop & mobile):** 👉 https://omgorigold-maker.github.io/itchy-snake/

Classic snake, except every 4 seconds it stops to scratch its snaketicals.

## Why?
Every 4 seconds, the **Itch Meter** maxes out, the snake pauses for a quick `*scritch scritch*` break, blushes with pure relief, and then gets right back to business.

## How to Play
- **Desktop:** Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to steer. Press **Space** or **P** to pause, **R** to restart.
- **Mobile:** **Swipe** on the board or tap the on-screen arrows to steer. **Press and hold** the board to pause (let go to jump right back in—*"It's okay, you can scratch your balls too."*).
- **Difficulty:** Starts nice and relaxed, then speeds up the more apples you eat.

Don't hit the walls. Don't bite yourself. Respect the itch.
